StepView  —  Modeling Step View Option

StepView

StepView is a toggle command for controlling whether intermediate-step shapes are displayed during modeling.

Purpose

Used to toggle the display of intermediate modeling step shapes for verification. When enabled, retains auxiliary shapes from each modeling step for detailed inspection; when disabled, focuses on final result and cleans up intermediate geometry.

How to Use

  1. Type StepView in the Rhino command line and press Enter.
  2. Toggle Step_View_On On or Off.
  3. Run a modeling command to verify whether intermediate-step display is active.

Options

OptionDescription
Step_View_OnOn: retains more auxiliary shapes from intermediate modeling steps for verification. Off: focuses on the final result and cleans up intermediate shapes.

Related Commands

Used together with UMD and MVOption during modeling review.
