BP1 ~ BP5  —  BackPanel Modeling

BP1BP2BP3 BP4BP5

Automatically creates BackPanel 3D solids based on the glass surface (Glass Surface). BP1 ~ BP5 each correspond to BackPanel types 1–5 pre-configured in the BpOpt panel.

Purpose

Used to quickly create spandrel (back panel) 3D solids across multiple faces via type shortcuts (BP1-BP5). Supports Distance or Section split methods, enabling fast placement of varied BackPanel specifications by type, with optional control over distance basis or section division.

How to Use

  1. Set the BackPanel type (thickness, offset distance, bend type, etc.) in the BpOpt panel first.
  2. Type BP1 (or BP2–BP5) in the Rhino command line and press Enter.
  3. Click on the glass surface to select the area where the BackPanel will be created.
  4. Optionally choose Distance or Section as the split method.
  5. Press Enter to finish — solids are created on the BackPanel layer.

Options

OptionDescription

SectionSplits BackPanel faces based on Section data.

Result

ItemDescription

ShapeA 3D solid composed of Sheet, Panel, and Insulation layers is created at the offset distance from the glass surface.
