Gmd  —  Bay Surface Modeling

Gmd

For each bay of a wireframe unit, creates one face on the plane formed by the mullion and transom lines (the member centreline position). The faces are placed on the Glass_XX layer.

Purpose

The preparation step for commands that start from a bay face — back panel, vent, grill and sheet. Those commands run on the selected Glass_XX faces.
For glass, use GFace, which places the face at the glass line drawn in the sections. In a unit that mixes glass bays with back panel or sheet bays, use GFace for the glass bays and Gmd for the rest.

Preparation

ItemDescription
WireframeThe mullion (M_Center) and transom (T_Center) lines must be in one group. A group with no M or T line is treated as not selected.

How to Use

  1. Type Gmd in the Rhino command line and press Enter.
  2. Select the wireframe unit (group) and press Enter. Several units can be selected together.
  3. A face is created on the Glass_XX layer for each bay, and the number of faces is shown in the command line.

Example

Rules

ItemDescription
Finding baysBays are found among lines lying in the same plane. Bays with five or more sides and units bent into several planes get one face per bay.
Skipped baysA closed loop not bounded by both mullions and transoms is skipped, and the count is reported in the command line. Lines that share no plane with other lines are counted as well.
Running againGmd does not delete existing faces. To make them again, clear them first with GMDC.
Glass with GSGS1 – GS9 also accept Glass_XX faces. If the same bay's Glass_XX1 – Glass_XX5 faces (GFace) are selected together, the Glass_XX face is skipped so the glass is not made twice.

Related Commands

CommandDescription
GFaceGlass position surface — creates Glass_XX1 – Glass_XX5 at the glass lines in the sections.
GMDCClear glass surfaces — deletes Glass_XX and Glass_XX1 – Glass_XX5 faces.
ShFaceSheet bay surface — creates sheet faces in bays that have S_Center1 lines.
GS1 – GS9Glass modeling — creates glass from the selected faces.
GsOptGlass type options — sets glass thickness and more.
