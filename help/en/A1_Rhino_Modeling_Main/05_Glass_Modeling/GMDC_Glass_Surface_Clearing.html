GMDC  —  Clear Glass Surfaces

GMDC

Deletes all surfaces on Glass_XX layers in the current document. Use this to start glass modeling from scratch.

Purpose

Used to clear all glass surfaces from Glass_XX layers and restart glass modeling from the beginning. Clean up existing results before remodeling when changing glass types or division methods.

How to Use

  1. Type GMDC in the Rhino command line and press Enter.
  2. Executes immediately without any selection.
  3. All surfaces on Glass_XX layers are deleted and the display is refreshed.

Example

Deletion Target

ItemDescription
Target LayersAll surface objects on layers matching the Glass_XX pattern.
Deletion ScopeEntire current document — deleted regardless of selection state.
ExcludedObjects on layers other than Glass_XX are unaffected.
Caution: All glass surfaces are deleted immediately upon execution. Use Rhino's Undo (Ctrl+Z) to revert.
