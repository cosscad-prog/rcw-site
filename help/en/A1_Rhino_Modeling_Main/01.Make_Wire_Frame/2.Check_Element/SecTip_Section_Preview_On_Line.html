SecTip  —  Section Preview on Lines

SecTip

Rest the mouse for a moment (0.3 seconds) on an M_Center, T_Center or R_Center line, and a small box (100 px) next to the cursor shows the section at that spot. The sections of overlapping lines and attach lines (AT_Center) there are drawn on top, so you can check how the members meet before modeling.

The preview is on from the start. The SecTip command turns it off and on.

How to Use

  1. Move the mouse onto an M_Center, T_Center or R_Center line of the wireframe and rest it there for a moment.
  2. The section box appears next to the cursor. Move the mouse along the line and the box follows.
  3. While the box is shown, moving to another line or another bay switches straight to the section there.
  4. Leave the line or press a mouse button and the box disappears.
  5. To turn the preview off, run SecTip. Run it again to turn it back on. The setting is kept when Rhino restarts.

What the Box Shows

ItemDescription
Section of the lineThe Section group found by the line's layer name (registered with SSP), shown as it was drawn.
Overlapping linesThe sections of M, T and R lines lying parallel at the same spot (within 0.5 mm) are drawn on top at their places in the model. Example: the two half mullions of neighboring units at a unit boundary.
Attach linesThe sections of the AT_Center lines covering the cursor spot. Attach lines differ from bay to bay, so only the ones of the bay under the cursor are drawn.
OrientationSections are placed by the same rule UMD uses to build members (the line direction and the WVector direction).
Name linesShort section names are listed below the box (for example M_Center3r → MC3r, AT_Center-MC3-Vi → ATC-MC3-Vi). Names not registered with SSP are dimmed as name - no SSP.
The box is drawn on the screen only. It creates no objects in the document, so nothing is saved in the file, printed or recorded for Undo.
A line not registered with SSP shows no section (an empty box with no SSP). A line without a WVector is treated as having the same orientation as the line under the cursor.

Related Commands

CommandDescription
SSPRegisters Section groups by layer name (the sections the preview looks up)
WVectorCreates Wire Vectors (line direction and the orientation used to place sections)
LineLayerDotTextShows the layer names of picked lines on the screen
