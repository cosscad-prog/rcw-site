ShFd  —  Sheet Fabrication Drawing

ShFd

Draws a fabrication drawing for each representative sheet placed by ShTable. A front view with dimensions, top and right sections, an isometric view, an unfolded view, and a table of the attached profiles are drawn in one cell.

Purpose

Use it to produce the shop drawings of the sheets. The views and dimensions are taken directly from the model, so the shape of the model (corner joints, holes, sloped sides) appears in the drawing as it is.

Workflow: WVector → SSP → ShMD → Sht → ShNo → ShList → ShTable → ShFd
The sheet commands rely on the S_Center1 line drawn in each section, on registered sections (SSP), and on a wireframe with direction data (WVector).

How to Use

  1. Run ShNo and then ShTable to create the table.
  2. Type ShFd in the Rhino command line and press Enter.
  3. If needed, choose the views with the command-line options (Section_On, ISO_On, Unfold_On).
  4. Select the representative sheets in the table and press Enter. Press Enter without selecting anything to draw every sheet in the table.

Example

Views

ViewPositionContent
Front viewAt the representative sheet2D lines of the sheet seen from the front (visible and hidden lines). The 3D copy is removed after drawing.
Horizontal sectionAbove the front viewCut at mid height. Dimensions of overall length, depth, thickness, and return length.
Vertical sectionRight of the front viewCut at mid width. The same dimensions.
Isometric viewLeft of the front view3D view including hidden lines.
Unfolded viewBelow the front viewThe model unfolded at its bend lines. Bend lines and holes are shown and the overall size is written as UNFOLD width x height.
Profile tableBelow the unfolded viewName, fabrication shape, length, and quantity of the attached profiles. Not drawn when there is no profile.

Front View Dimensions

The front view shows the size of the panel first; dimensions of the attached profiles and holes are secondary. The dimension rows, from the panel outward, are as follows.

RowDescription
1. Bars and holesOn each side, a chain from corner to bar (or hole) to corner. On a sloped side it is measured along the side.
2. Panel cornersPosition of the corner where a side ends on a sloped panel. Omitted for a rectangle because it equals the overall dimension.
3. OverallOverall width and height of the panel face. With an outside return, one more row gives the size including the wings.
Sloped sideA sloped side with nothing attached gets its true length.

Unfolded View

ItemDescription
MethodThe model is split into the panel, the folded leg of each side, and the returns, and laid flat at the bend lines. Corner shapes and holes of the model appear as they are.
LengthsNo bend allowance is applied. Folded leg = depth − thickness, return = return length − thickness.
Line typesLines on the front side are solid; lines on the back side and bend lines are on the hidden-line layer. Where two lines overlap, only the visible one is kept.
DimensionsOverall width and height. A rectangular sheet also gets chain dimensions of the legs and the panel face.
Sloped sheetsTrapezoids and parallelograms are unfolded as well.

Attached Profile Table

ItemDescription
TableOne table per profile name. Bars with the same length, end shape, and holes share a row.
Fabrication shapeSection shape and a sketch of the bar. The sketch is not drawn to length — it shows whether the ends are cut at 45° and whether there are holes.
LengthLongest side of the bar (mm).
QuantityNumber of bars attached to one sheet.

Layers and Notes

ItemDescription
LayersVisible lines Sheet_Fab, hidden and bend lines Sheet_Fab_Hidden, dimensions and text Dims.
Text sizeFollows the text size of the current dimension style.
Drawing againDrawing removes the 3D copy from the table. To draw again, run ShTable first.
Drawing planeThe drawing lies on the drawing plane seen in the Front view.
