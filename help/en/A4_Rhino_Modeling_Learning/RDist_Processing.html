R-Dist  —  Parallel Slope Cut at Both Ends

Fab_ID = 6.R-Dist

Fab ID setting that cuts the top end and the bottom end of a member with parallel slopes. The slope is set by a distance (height difference, mm), not by an angle.

Purpose

Use it for members such as sleeves whose top and bottom ends lean slightly in the same direction. 5.R45 cuts the two ends with facing 45-degree slopes (a trapezoid); 6.R-Dist cuts them with parallel slopes (a parallelogram).

Meaning of the Distance

ItemDescription
Bottom endStarts at the lowest corner on one side of the depth and rises by the distance at the other side.
Top endParallel to the bottom end: starts at the highest corner on the other side and drops by the distance.
Overall lengthUnchanged. Only one edge on each end becomes shorter by the distance.
DepthMeasured on the member itself (its width along the direction recorded on the centerline). The same distance gives a different angle on members of different depth — 10 over a depth of 100 is about 5.7 degrees, 10 over a depth of 50 is about 11.3 degrees.
Negative valueA value such as -10 slopes the other way.
FormulaA formula such as 5+5 is accepted.

Application Criteria

  1. In the Pname window, set the Fab ID of the section to 6. R-Dist.
  2. Enter the distance in the box immediately to the right of the Fab ID list. The box is enabled only for number 6.
  3. Save, then model with UMD.
  4. If the slope leans the wrong way, change the sign of the distance.

The same value can be edited on the RCW Section tab of the Rhino Properties panel, in the same position (the box to the right of Fab_ID).

Note: Changing the Fab ID to another number does not erase the distance; the box is only disabled. When you return to number 6 the value is still there.
Caution:
  • With number 6, Pname does not save while the distance is empty or 0. If a section became number 6 another way (for example in Dtable) and has no distance, modeling leaves the member uncut and reports "Fab_ID 6 (R-Dist): could not cut the ends of …".
  • Ends that overlap other members are trimmed again by later modeling steps. If the overlap is larger than the distance, no slope remains on the final member.
  • Applies only to members extruded from the section group. Accessories inserted as blocks do not read the Fab ID.
