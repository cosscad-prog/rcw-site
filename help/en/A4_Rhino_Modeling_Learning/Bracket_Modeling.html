Bracket Modeling

8.M_Bracket 9.T_Bracket 8.Bracket-Inter

Brackets are generated using the intersection of Mullion side elements and Transom side elements.

Purpose

Used when creating a Bracket shape where a Mullion side Surface or PolyLine meets a Transom side PolyLine or Surface. The Bracket shape is formed based on the intersection location of the two elements.

Requirements

ItemDescription
Mullion SideRequires Surface or PolyLine.
Transom SideRequires PolyLine or Surface.
Layer ConditionThe two elements that intersect must be on the same Layer.

Creating Mullion Bracket

CombinationComposition
Combination 1Surface in Mullion Section Profile ID: 8.M_Bracket + PolyLine in Transom Section Material ID: 8.Bracket-Inter
Combination 2PolyLine in Mullion Section Material ID: 8.Bracket-Inter + Surface in Transom Section Profile ID: 8.M_Bracket

Creating Transom Bracket

CombinationComposition
Combination 1Surface in Mullion Section Profile ID: 9.T_Bracket + PolyLine in Transom Section Material ID: 8.Bracket-Inter
Combination 2PolyLine in Mullion Section Material ID: 8.Bracket-Inter + Surface in Transom Section Profile ID: 9.T_Bracket
