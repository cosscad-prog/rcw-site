F45  —  Front View 45 Degree Machining

Fab_ID = 4.F45

Fab ID setting that applies 45-degree directional processing by finding the intermediate angle of two lines based on modeling Front View.

Purpose

Used when selective 45-degree processing is needed on members like Adapters due to glass thickness changes. Processing is performed by calculating the cutting angle based on where Mullion Line and Transom Line meet.

Application Criteria

  1. Specify the Fab ID of the member to be processed as 4.F45.
  2. Verify the surface where Mullion Line and Transom Line meet based on Model Front View reference.
  3. Calculate the cutting angle based on the intermediate angle between the two lines.
  4. Maintain the intermediate angle of the two lines even for non-right-angle conditions.
